So.. umm.. hi again(?) it's been so long since I wrote lmao.

Actually, it isn't that long, but if you compared the gaps between my previous posts, this currently holds the longest gap between them. So what was I up to? Boring answer is I was migrating this site, but that doesn't sounds cool enough. Let me re-phrase it: *I was searching for perfection, found enlightenment instead*.

Some of you might know that in the past, this site ran on [Notion](https://www.notion.com/), there's nothing wrong with that, in fact I really likes it, it might have a bit of learning curve to get used to, but once you get the hang of it, it's really powerful, even able to host a static site for free, crazy! So.. yeah I'd pretty much recommend it to anyone that's looking for some productivity tools to tinker with lol.

*"If Notion's that good, then why you migrate?"* As you can already tell (with the modest layout lol), this time I tried to built my own site from the ground up (more or less), as for the why.. let me give you some context.

I can't exactly pinpoint it, but for the longest time, I always have this urge(?) of having as little attachments as possible. My assumption is maybe I'm just THAT scared of losing things, it translated to this: how about someday the platform decides to actually gate it behind a paywall, or what if someday the whole thing went down with all of my *thoughts*, that ruminates over and over until I finally reach this state.

It's so funny that Notion's too good, I was scared they'll go premium soon lol, but it's true.

Suuuureeee.. that doesn't make all the sense (at least for now), but since when do I think clearly? Having said that, my current config is also not really *attachment-less*, in fact it's so far from that still (most of the savvy out there running all of this from their own homelab and all of those shenanigans lol), but I think little steps matters.

Then my journey began, *"surely building a simple static website is easy enough no?"* If it's you maybe.. maybe it'll only take hours, but for my dumb ass? I got humbled in like 30 minutes. Once a big grand aspiration such as imitating my old Notion setup ended up me having a mental breakdown wrestling with CSS.

After a couple of moments feeling defeated, I decided to ditch the fancy *nested layout* and go for content-heavy style, after all, layouting this bunch of text would be easier right? Yes in a sense, but I stopped for another minute, *"I need some inspirations"*, I thought, it then lead to a totally different endeavor.

Let me try to summarize it. Searching for some of these content-heavy websites widen my perspective, when you go all minimal but your content, surely the content itself was the one carried your website right? It turns out just like I expect, they're *insane*, some of them have this very optimized web that only KBs of size, some of them having a very unusual layout imitating a console-based OS, some of them wrote an advance math theory, some of them having bunch of obscure tech stack, it was.. overwhelming to say the least.

As much as it's overwhelming, it's also very inspiring, I WANT to have it like them, it's so cool! And just like that I was on whole 'nother kind of rabbit hole.

Hour turned to days, in a daze, I catch myself lacking, lacking the thing I want to do from the very first hour, that is to actually migrate my thoughts and make it *live*, instead, I was deep in my own sense of inadequacy, I was busy ruminating the fact that I am subpar both at layouting and the weight of the substance.

It happened again, I was chasing after perfection, it was horrible, even considering to abandon all of this.

.

.

.

One day, when browsing through all of those cool websites, I happened to clicked on this one website, the name caught my attention, it was written in kanji, it was cool, the guy was apparently one of the maintainer of core service that Anki used, the usual tech-savvy programmer.

After bit of scrolling, I saw this link that sits in the middle of others in foreign characters, it said "blog", I clicked it, and you know what.. It led me back to a site made with Notion (lmao). It was real eye opener, the guy even set a cheesy scene from an old-school anime as the banner, it was very *unserious*, that jolted me awake.

In the first place, my site was for me, for me to express myself, for me to have an outlet, and for me to experiment. I was so caught up in searching for the perfect website THEN proceed to make it live, while in fact I can just make do with current state and add to it [iteratively](#work-in-iterations).

From there, my progress made a sudden turn, now I'm less afraid to mess it up and focus on actually make it live, I picked up bunch of tricks here and there in the process, it was starting to looks like fun.

If you're able to read this, that meant I persevered. If I persevered, then I think you could too.

If you're in the middle of something that felt heavy, idk, maybe stops chasing after perfection, try that tips, tinker with those things, change up the perspective, roughly patch the blemishes.

Sure, you might ended up with a frankenstein-like garbage, but at least it's a thing that exist *plus* enlightenment rather than fantasized dreams *and* resentment.

I know I'd rather have the former.
