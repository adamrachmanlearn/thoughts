For the record, my kind of [calisthenics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calisthenics) isn’t those kinds who requires you to have superpower to hold yourself with.. idk.. [two fingers](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=two+fingers+planche), it’s much more modest than that actually.

<br>

The passing of my grandma was the initial trigger that kickstarted my exercise journey, the thought was simple: I don’t wanna die of same cause that is [cardiovascular disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiovascular_disease). Picked up [running](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Running) as my first ‘exercise’ and it’s still going until now (fortunately), but it’s story for another day.

<br>

Like the usual teenager who entered high school, I got interested in how-the-heck-can-I-make-my-body-more-muscular scheme, there it is, my first actual contact with the good ol’ [push up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Push-up). Sure it was already a *thing* from forever, everyone did it to impress the girls (or boys idk), but it’s starting that moment do I actually study the thing, plan my session and all, it was an honest effort to become more presentable (lol).

<br>

Fast forward to today, it’s still my main form of strength exercise, here are some of the things that make them just unbearably lovely from my point of view.

<br>

#### Low cost financially

<br>

Yea.. yeah, maybe I’m a cheapskate afterall, anyway.. it requires very little of your money, like extremely little, in some case even requires *nothing*. For the usual push-pull-leg scenario, if you’re fortunate enough to have a random hanging place/bar or a *strong* tree branch (emphasized on strong) on vicinity, you can just start. Do push up for your push workout, pull up (on that bar/tree) for your pull workout, and bodyweight squat for your legs.

<br>

#### Low cost friction-ally(?)

<br>

First of all, idk if i just made up a word, anyway.. did you’ve ever felt super lazy when it’s time to do your workout, maybe you need to drive to the gym, maybe you need to setup your workout mattress, search for that one spesific *follow-along* videos and whatnot. With calisthenics, that reduced to a very minimum amount, I legit often woke up, roll out of bed directly into my first push up working set (warming up is important btw), it’s just that easy.

<br>

#### Built functional strength

<br>

I know.. I know this one is a bit iffy, I know that many *macho* guys out there are striving for pure muscle mass, and maybe you’re right, for that purpose, going to the gym will prove to be much more efficient. But here me out, what if you actually don’t want that? What if having a functional strength with *aesthetic-enough* body is.. enough, like you don’t need to be that concerned with your protein intake, about how your idk.. left posterior delts a bit smaller than the right.

<br>

Sure having that big muscular body is cool and all, but often times we forgot that it requires a lot of time and maintenance, a mere mortal like me (or you) might not have the luxury of putting our all mind into it. I swear calisthenics will not eat up that much of your time while still giving you like at least 80% the benefits. What a deal!

<br>

#### Highly scalable

<br>

Bear with me for using a corpo-lingo (lol), but it is! I also thought of it was only for the fit/young/short/talented people, and it’s not my (or your) fault! Social media makes it that calisthenics is all about doing [*planche*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planche_(exercise)) or [*muscle up*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle-up), while in fact its much more than that. Me myself start doing it *intently* after I came across [this video](https://youtu.be/0GsVJsS6474?si=vxOHhG9cUrYU737J) on YouTube (super nice guy btw, shout-out to Hampton my GOAT). I encourage you to see his routine [here](https://www.hybridcalisthenics.com/routine), guarantee there exist something for everybody on every fitness level.

<br>

There’s so much more to talk about, but these four things will suffice for the time being. Lastly if you have spare money to use, these *luxury* things could make your journey way more comfortable (not necessary).
- **Some form of pull up bar**: when your current bar/tree branch felt awful/unsafe (trust me it’s worth it to invest here lol)
- **Pair of dumbbell**: used to isolate smaller muscle if you *care* about that, sometimes help with rehab exercises too.
- **Resistance band**: used to assist you when learning new move (e.g. pull up), sometimes help with rehab exercises too.

<br>

And, there you have it.
