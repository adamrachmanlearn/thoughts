I’ve always thought the reason you do certain things is something so serious.

Let us think about it, you born in this world with limited amount of time, limited amount of opportunities, of course you will do things that’s strictly benefit you in some grand notion right? It is only logical and for the longest time I also thought of it this way. But this kinds of thinking affected me in some ways.. it made me scared.. scared that the things i did was in fact ‘meaningless’.

Earlier in my life I was always told to be a bright kid, somehow it changed me into someone who value only results.. exceptional results at that, sure, the amount of intelligent you need to cruise through childhood isn’t that high, fortunately (or not) it was also a breeze for me. Tests was easy, got high ranking in class was another tuesday, you get the gist.

Then everything *changed*.

I get older, *we* get older. Things once was easy to get, now not so much, getting the hang of things took longer than usual, solving problems was starting to turned into existential crisis, as for getting a rank in class? Yeah good luck with that..

Its like some admin turned up the difficulty lever all of a sudden (lol), but in fact (at least I’m assuming) it is that *I’m* the one who are too used to everything being easy.. the second it felt that im not progressing, I dropped it, no indication of getting better in near future? Leaving it for another. The thought of starting on something that is not giving tell tale sign of becoming good at that was terrifying, you guessed it.. I’ve become a big procrastinator.

The woe of becoming a procrastinator.

Where everyone navigating their way through this world, I’m standing right here, as if someone who lost their compass in the middle of forest, its a *gross* feeling. Fortunately in some random day I had encountered this very thought (this was also one of the things that made me created this tiny corner on the Internet) that i want to share with.. honestly dont know.. maybe for myself in the end.

Here they are if you want my two cents :)

#### Doing things for the sake of doing

What if we do something for.. doing something, no results, no achievements, no underlying motives, it’s simply.. what we do.

Earlier this week i came across a short video about [Yuki Tsunoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuki_Tsunoda), apparently the guy was F1 driver for 2025 [Red Bull Racing team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Bull_Racing), his teammate was [Max Verstappen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Verstappen). Now let me be clear, I am not someone who watch F1, but little scroll action told me that Yuki was considered one of the worst driver, while Max is quite the opposite.

That ultimately got me thinking, “Is this a bad joke or something?”, like why bother eat, train, sleep, live the life of an F1 driver (I heard it’s quite strict) when you just going to finish last place? Even people closest to you is worlds above your skill level, didn’t it makes you upset?

Then it expanded.

For every talent like [Christiano Ronaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo) or [Lionel Messi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionel_Messi), there’s millions of players out there, living the ultra-disciplined lifestyle of a football/soccer athlete, trained hard everyday and everything, but it is also do not free them from the possibility that.. maybe they will not achieve it, maybe they’ll ended up average, or worst.. they feel they did not find the meaning of it all and leave. This train of thoughts ultimately spreaded far and wide to other field of athletes, professionals, and finally.. the people around me.

“Why they work so hard? Why they do what they do? What is the secret?”. That was all my brain could think of, then I got reminded of Yuki, to where it all started.. The fact that there’s only around 20 F1 drivers, if being dead last made you a failure, then the rest 7.9 billions of society should not be awake the next day (I know the 7.9 bil populations would not all want to be an F1 driver, but you get my point lol).

Just do it.

Seriously. Just. Do. It, pick an identity and do all the things that you would do.. If you’re a student, then study, learn the hard things you’ve been avoiding, learn for the sake of learning, who cares if you ended up failing that test, the important thing is you *did* what you do. If you’re an aspiring artist, just create something man.. you could always patch the blemishes, you can’t patch things that isn’t exist, if you’re a runner, just run the miles, who cares about paces and PBs, just savor the fact that you *did* the run, others can wait until then.

Do things for the sake of doing it, cause at the end of the day, everyone is *winging* it, I strongly believe that people still not figured things out, it’s all confusing, so might as well do the things you want to do.

Go out there and do things! I sincerely hope you find many pleasant moments ahead.
