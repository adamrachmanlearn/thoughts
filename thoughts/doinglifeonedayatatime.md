*“Where do you see yourself in 5 years?”*. Ah yes, the one question that every recruiter do, no matter what.

Respectfully, I *hate* that question, I *hate* it very much!

Picture this, all of a sudden you *spawned* in the middle of ocean with bunch of random dudes, out of all people, it seems that only you that can’t swim. While some are okay with it, some even *thriving*, you, on the other hand, fighting that suffocation creeps in, wailing all your limbs to just *stay afloat*.

Then this one guy approaches, *“hey, what are you gonna do in the next hours? As for me, umm lets see.. think I’ll go to this spot, then that, then this, then finally maybe settle on this coast..”*.

Yeah.. haha, great for you! Wouldn’t it be great if I can *at least* stay f\*ck\*ng afloat to be able to a even plan ahead?

Life is wonderful, to certain extent I can’t refute that, endless possibilities, myriad of experiences, all the good stuff, but unfortunately, there’s a lot of people who can’t even fathom that fact, cause we simply struggle to just stay *afloat*, our limbs are in a constant motion to kept us afloat, to keep is *here*, we do try, *earnestly*.

Some of us *tried*.

.

.

.

I don’t know if this will ever reach someone, but in a case you, who are reading this felt something similar, here’s my proposition that you can try.

#### Do life one day at a time

I often hear some says *“look at the big picture..”*, you do you if that helps, but personally, that made me even more *blue*, all I can see was *doom and gloom*, then I tried the opposite, let me reduce the scope as much as I can, for me, a day is suffice, but that’s not fixed, you can use your own metric, whether its one month, one week, one day, even, one hour (heck, you can even do 15 minutes).

For the next [time metric] you chose, just focus on doing what you need to do. I do believe every single one of us *know* what we need to do, it’s just sometimes starting is terrifying, it has so much power over us. But what if we can just do it for as long as [time metric] allows, no pressure.

Sure, after that one month/one week/one day/one hour/one.. *moment* is finished, life will not magically get better, it could be possibly worst (lol), but you *still here*, that mattered the most, you’ve won at that point.

Now, maybe you still have some in the tank to start another [*iteration*](#work-in-iterations), then another, then.. another?

You’ve floated longer than ever, and maybe.. just maybe, you can float.. forever.
