It isn't to achieve something, some profound financial stand I hold, or something superficial, it's as simple as, *"I don't want to deal with the dramatic events that follows lack-of-money scenario"*.

Anyway, here's my point. **You are NOT a seer**. You can't predict what would happen in the future, in this context, obviously talking about money. Many people, once they have a steady stream, like pocket money from parents, a monthly allowance from relatives, salary from your job, etc. They get this illusions of the never-ending continuity, where in fact, nothing, and I mean NOTHING would guarantee sh\*t.

<br>

This just happened to me, now, I'm not the subject for this, more like a victim (lol). There's this one family member who has steady high income, they irrefutably loves the family and don't meant any ill, and vice versa, we all can't survive this world without them, it's just one *thing* that this person has, they felt like they *can* predict the future, in this case, *using* practically all their money into something that isn't *liquid*, now the financial theory aside, I just want to emphasized this simple rule, yet ridiculously often forgotten by so many people.

<br>

Set aside your money. Seriously, no matter how much, at least you have something, sure, you might be just grinning right now, or even thinking that the measly amount you set isn't gonna help anybody. It will. It'll make a difference, it's not about the amount, it's your constant choice to set them aside, it'll reinforced your habit (if you could say) each time you do it. As for the amount, it'll pile up, trust me.

<br>

It's not even gonna help you only when the time comes (it'll come), it actively provides some peace of mind from *day one*, you wouldn't as worry when life *happens*, it'll unironically improves your quality of life, just like when you buy that new air conditioner, a new comfortable shoes, or that annoying neighbor who blasted loud music at 2AM finally realizing their mistake and keep quiet at night.
