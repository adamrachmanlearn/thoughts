My earliest memory of my surroundings was this small veranda on grandma’s house, there was this big [sapodilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manilkara_zapota) tree in front of it, it was ordinary, modest, yet serene neighborhood.

<br>

It *was* my whole world, my whole horizon. Life was a matter of helping grandma inside, or running around below the tree outside, even when accompanying her to a local shop, it was a big adventure, can’t imagine if I lose her footsteps back then, maybe I’d cry, thought that I’d never see *home* again.

<br>

Then life happened (it always does, jeez), I somehow got admitted to nearest kindergarten, if memory serves me right, it’s around two or three kilometers from grandma’s. I kid you not, it was terrifying, it’s like I’m walking nonstop to get there, seeing various new place that seems foreign, I even remember the time when I got chased by bunch of geese (most diabolical animal imo btw). It was whole different world.

<br>

That whole sequence then repeated throughout my life, it happened again and again. When I inevitably progressed to elementary school, then junior high, then senior high, then college, then working, every time this things happened, I also kinda forced to take in new surroundings, new world, new.. horizon.

<br>

Places that used to be foreign, now, little-by-little became something that you missed, something that close to *home*, place that you know like the back of your hand. The process often so subtle, I can’t even tell sometimes.

<br>

This, at least for me, is strange. I never expected any of this, but somehow life happened, and in the blink of eyes, I was doing chores in a distance that was mind-boggling far for younger me. That got me thinking, if someone like me already covered this much, how big is the horizon of someone who *deliberately* doing it, think someone who likes to travel, to explore, new neighborhood, new city, new land, new country, or even new continent.

<br>

Now, I’m not someone who look forward to the future, but I can’t lie, I’m quite curious at how much bigger my horizon got by the time I’m at my *curtain call*.
