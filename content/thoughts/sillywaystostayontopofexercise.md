<br>

#### I'm a shounen protagonist!

<br>

For context [shounen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Dnen_manga) is a term usually found in manga or anime indicating that their target audience is adolescent boys. Personally I think one of the most common tropes are the defeat of our protagonist, went through some kinds of training arc, then finally able to overcome their adversaries.

<br>

Yeahh.. yeahh it's so *cheesy*, but you've read the title, so let's continue.

<br>

When I'm low on *willpower*, I usually remember those epic scenes, re-enacting-it in my head, then usually I'll have enough hype(?) to push through that session. Sure this won't work for all people, because fortunately (or not) I've always the type to experience emotion on another depth, like if I'm not careful, I can bawls my eyes out in the middle of my long run (happened before smh).

<br>

So, maybe you'll have some characters you admire, maybe from animes, books, movies, whatever. It can also very be a real person who you looked up to, what do you think they'll do in your situation? I found this weirdly motivating.

<br>

#### Study how your body works

<br>

Okay, this one might not be very silly, but my next point would link to this, so bear with me.

<br>

Let me just share my own anecdote. Up until relatively recently, believe it or not, I was not really into running (relative to my current state, it IS world's apart). I mean, sure I enjoy running, even I already did like 6 sessions per week, and it's years already since I first started.

<br>

But deep down, I don't feel the *passion*, that I had at the time with [calisthenics](#why-i-love-calisthenics), it's more like a routine than something that I do wholeheartedly.

<br>

Until I learn the concept of *aerobic base*, it was a rabbit hole that made me re-structure my whole running regime, literally. To give the tip of the iceberg, aerobic base is your body's ability to use fats as a fuel, for an average person who live a sedentary lifestyle, this ability, or you could say that, their aerobic base isn't so good, so their body's main fuel is sugar, and you might know that our bodies could only store so much sugar until it drops, unlike fats that is *plenty* (yeah, even those in your face, abdomen, and thighs lol).

<br>

TL:DR is, when you have good aerobic base, you're somewhat like a phone with good battery, and vice versa, I'd imagine you'll prefer to charge your phone once a day, instead of needing to look for a wall outlet every 30 minutes right? (not scientific btw, it's just my interpretation lol).

<br>

#### Imagine the thing that you consumed is an enemy you must defeat

<br>

First of all, I don't encourage a negative body outlook, if you have something serious like [body dysmorphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_dysmorphic_disorder), please do yourself some favor and properly consult to a professional, thank you.

<br>

Once you know how your body works, you'll naturally know how they will process the things that you've consumed, cause let's be real, calorie intake is OFTEN the actual things that will *move the needle*, because you can only burn *so much* (I remember running for almost 2 hours and only able to burn like.. 600 ish lol).

<br>

Anyway, I'm often finding myself hyped up to do those pull-ups right after (not literally, you can puke) a big meals, like *"Okay, mr. fried rice, let us see, how well you'll manage after I did my reps"*. Because our muscle is the primary organ that will sucks out *blood sugar*, not letting them fester to finally stored as fats, I know this will sounds weird but I'm sure you get the gist.

<br>

It's not antagonizing foods, it's just doing *something* to them, instead of just letting them in for *free*.
