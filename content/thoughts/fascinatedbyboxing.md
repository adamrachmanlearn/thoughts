#### Footwork, footwork, footwork

<br>

Okay, let me be super honest, I think I wrote all of this only to be able to yap about footwork (lol).

<br>

Yeah, punching the sh\*t out of your opponent is cool and all, landing all of those combos you've practiced on the heavy bag, the one that gives you those victory in a *match*.

<br>

Me personally though, especially lately, been very fired up when people pulls out the most unhinged footwork, the one that actually moves them from one spot to another in a *blitz*, the one that actually makes you wanna replay it, *"how the heck did he evades that?"*, or *"can't believe he closed the gap and landed a body shot that instant"* type thing.

<br>

Even the 'boring' footwork requires you to drill it day in and day out, not to mention the enormous *aerobic capability* to actually sustain them throughout the match. For me personally, a good footwork impresses me more than any strikes landed.
