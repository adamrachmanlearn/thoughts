If someone asks me what a [gacha games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gacha_game) is, maybe I’ll put it this way, it’s a game where the main focus is collecting stuffs, it could be characters, weapon, costumes, etc.

<br>

Here’s the catch, everytime you want something, there’s usually no direct way to get it, you need to go through the process of getting *randomized* items, yup.. in a way you need to gamble your way into getting it (lol). Back then, it used to be more *scary*, like there wasn’t any guardrails that [you’ll kept getting the things that you don’t want](https://youtu.be/teBFHO-vQn8?si=pHAsaIiloVSZKQlV), but these days, I think majority of gacha games are forced to have some kind of guardrails by laws, but don’t quote me on that.

<br>

There’s that, but today I want to talk to spesifically weird thing that happened to me. Aside from the main focus of the game, which is gambling, I notice these games usually are free to play, which I assume are their way to gather more player then the more player also would like it, then justified to spend some real money inside.

<br>

One of the characteristic also, while the new stuffs on the banner (where you do those randomized pulls) cycles quite fast, the free resources you get from being a free-to-play player is definitely not enough to keep doing those pulls, so if you want to get the latest stuff, you need to spend real money (again).

<br>

Me, as a free-to-play player, was succumbed to same fate, which I always ended up lacking the funds to do anything meaningful, this is true to both of my first gacha games, but the third time I do it, I have this mindset of, *“I’m done being played by these companies, let see if I can optimize my resources even better now”*, and which I did, I plan carefully about the characters that I want, I held back on non-substantial stuffs they offer at the time, those kinds of things.

<br>

The system that was meant to suffocate the person to spend, unintentionally was turned into a low-risk high-reward environment for me to manage my resources, and the crazy things, after doing this for years inside the game, those mindset got carried into my personal finance space.

<br>

Now I do manage my money carefully, holding back on meaningless stuffs, and when the actual big stuffs is out, I can, and able to make those purchase without me feeling like I always lacking.
