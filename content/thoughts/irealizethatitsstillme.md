So, in [this post](#slow-down-physically), I've talked about how conciously choosing to slow down physically could bring more peace to your day. Turns out, there's so much more in that area, that I could aim to improve.

<br>

You can bet, that after writing that one, I've tried my best to slow down, It's an idea that I've pitched after all, If at least one person were to do it, it gotta be me. Even with this opportunity, I'm gonna emphasize it one more time, it actually helps, I've become more peaceful, not hurried, less anxiety, but I've also learned that, it's not all there's to it.

<br>

...

<br>

Brand new day, it's as ordinary as any day could get, I'm ready to depart for work, I even remember the sun was more forgiving than usual, meaning there's a good chance I'd arrive at the office without sweating bullets (thank god), boy, did I not prepared for all the things that *life* would threw that day. It was anything but peaceful.

<br>

I'll try to not shove you with all the unnecessary details, but thinking about it, I've got a bad sleep to begin with, then the rest of the *shits* happened on commute, as per usual. The sleep, I somehow could *take* it, because it was not the first time I've dealt with bad sleep, the commute though, was awful, and most of these things had something in common, or, at least I've assumed so, It was *others* who's at fault.

<br>

Let me tell you this one, I've careful with my steps, queued patiently, in the corner of my eyes I already knew that a guy wanted to cut the queue, which of course I've anticipated it, even trying to understand him, what made me furious was the guy literally *push* me to the side aggresively like I'm the one who's tryna cut the line, like damn bro chill out lmao.

<br>

Things like this happened again and again with slight changes in all of them, but the core idea was the same, *"I've been careful and meticulous with my gesture, yet all of these people seemingly act like they wanted to bait me into a rage"*. I've seriously considered to just go back to my usual way and *fight* these people with the same attitude, like *"lets push each other until one of use falls from the platform shall we?"* lol. That day, I was so stressed out, I ended up catching a common cold at the end of the day.

<br>

Then I remember a notion in stoicism that talks about how all things come in a neutral nature, and I, am the one who attached stories to these things, upon further contemplation, I decided to once again test a theory onto the very commute I'd have next day. Sure enough I'd tried to remember the adage again and again in my head, I essentially brainwashed myself into the teaching.

<br>

Surprisingly, the thing worked. I got cutted from a queue, naturally I felt bad because I was supposed to be on *that place* by now, I was supposed to get that seat, I was supposed to arrived by now. I am late.

<br>

Late? late for what, seriously, this could become a post by it's own, but again, late for what?

<br>

So, instead of cursing on how these people seemingly trying to sabotage me, I tried to think that I was in a location that I was meant to be, okay some random dude made me miss a bus, let it be, I was supposed to miss the bus from the start, I was never meant to take that bus. Someone being rude to me, okay fine, I'll try to just *experience* it, then move on, I'll try to not attach any stories on how someone should be more considerate, or he should have bad morning for him to be lashing his anger to me, nothing. It just happened then I moved on.

<br>

Sure, there's some counter-argument that can be raised to this, but it works, at least for me. And at the end of the day, I'll choose my peace of mind over anything really.

<br>

I realized, that all of this obnoxious, external things that seemingly here to hurt me, is my own stories, my own narration that I'd attach to things that I think of an inconvenience, I'm not sure why I did that, why common people did that, maybe we attached it for justification, to allows ourselves to fall into our rage and lashes out on people, and we could see that, by doing it, it'll create a vicious cycle that made everyone's day ruined.

<br>

So note to self, the next time you experienced an *external inconvenience*, pause, try to dissect the things from the stories you've attached to it, maybe it's not the thing at all, maybe it's you, it is I, that causes it.
